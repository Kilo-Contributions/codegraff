# Codegraff v0.0.302.16

This release adds one term to graff's license, for funded companies. graff
itself is unchanged from v0.0.302.15.

## Funded companies need a commercial license

From this version on, a company that, together with its affiliates, has
raised more than US$500,000 from investors or lenders (equity, convertible
notes, SAFEs or similar instruments, at any valuation) may use graff only
under a commercial license granted jointly by the Licensors. Use includes
running graff on the company's own computers, servers, CI and networks, and
making it available to its people or users over a network.

- A company that becomes a funded company may continue its existing use for
  30 days, within which it gets a commercial license or stops using graff.
- Individuals using graff for themselves, and organizations that are not
  funded companies, keep using it under the AGPL-3.0.
- Versions up to and including v0.0.302.15 keep the license they shipped
  with.
- Components under their own license, such as the Apache-2.0 SDKs, keep it;
  a copy of graff they include or download is under graff's license.

This is a summary; the
[LICENSE](https://github.com/justrach/codegraff/blob/v0.0.302.16/LICENSE)
text is what applies (#1447). For a commercial license, support or
deployment help, contact
[rach@standardharness.com](mailto:rach@standardharness.com).
